# Club Friday Logo

## Meaning

The Club Friday logo represents an intergenerational sports community built on justice, unity, respect, empathy, and harmony.

Three members of different ages stand together around a shared sports ball. Their arrangement shows that young people, adults, and older people all have a valued place in the club. The figures are intentionally gender-neutral so the logo represents the community as a whole.

## Main Elements

### Three members

- The younger member is on the left.
- The adult member is in the center.
- The older member is on the right.
- Their equal visual importance represents dignity across generations.
- Their open, rounded forms suggest cooperation rather than competition between people.

### Shared sports ball

The central ball is an abstract multi-sport symbol, not a football. Its curved panels suggest movement and participation across many activities, including:

- Cricket
- Football (soccer)
- Kabaddi (Ha-Du-Du)
- Hockey
- Badminton
- Volleyball
- Local athletics
- Other healthy sports

Using one universal ball keeps the logo simple and prevents it from favoring one sport over another.

### Outer arc

The navy arc protects and frames the community. It represents justice, responsibility, fairness, and the commitment to stand together for what is right.

### Lower joining arc

The teal arc connects the members visually. It represents unity, empathy, harmony, and mutual support.

### Arced lettering

The motto follows the upper arc, while the club name follows the lower arc. This creates a balanced badge and makes the name and message part of the same community circle.

## Motto

**JUSTICE - UNITY - RESPECT**

- **Justice** represents fairness and standing up for people who are treated unjustly.
- **Unity** represents collective action and harmony across ages and backgrounds.
- **Respect** represents dignity, empathy, fair play, and regard for every member.

These values are inspired by the central principle of Hilf al-Fudul: people joining together to defend justice, especially for those without strong protection or influence.

## Color Meaning

- **Navy `#17324D`** - justice, trust, protection, and responsibility. Used for the outer arc and the word JUSTICE.
- **Teal `#28705D`** - unity, empathy, harmony, and community. Used for the lower joining arc and the word UNITY.
- **Dark gold `#A86B00`** - dignity, value, and moral courage. Used for the shared ball and the word RESPECT.
- **Yellow `#F2B544`** - optimism, youth, and inclusion. Used for the younger member.
- **Coral `#E96B50`** - energy, confidence, and active participation. Used for the adult member.
- **Green `#4E9B83`** - growth, balance, and continuity. Used for the older member.
- **Slate `#243746`** - professionalism and strong identity. Used for CLUB FRIDAY.
- **Warm cream `#FFF4D6`** - openness and shared space. Used inside the ball.

The darker colors are used for small text and structural lines so the logo remains readable in print.

## Hand-Drawing Guidance

The logo is designed to be reproduced without specialist equipment:

1. Draw a broad upper arc to frame the three members.
2. Add three simple round heads and rounded body shapes: smaller on the left, larger in the center, and mature on the right.
3. Place one simple round sports ball beneath the figures.
4. Draw a few broad curved panels inside the ball instead of detailed sport-specific markings.
5. Add a lower connecting arc beneath the ball.
6. Place CLUB FRIDAY along the lower arc and JUSTICE - UNITY - RESPECT along the upper arc.
7. Use one dark outline when color is unavailable.

The logo should remain recognizable in color, black and white, embroidery, stamps, signage, and hand-painted versions. The background is transparent by design.
